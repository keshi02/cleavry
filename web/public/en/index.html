How it works

  1. Drop your image: drag a PNG or JPG into the canvas. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Remove the background: click "AI background removal". BiRefNet runs locally on WebGPU and isolates the subject.
  3. Touch up by hand: use the eraser, restore brush, or magic wand to fix anything the AI got wrong.
  4. Save as transparent PNG: hit "Save" to download the result. WebP and JPEG are also available.
  5. Split into pieces: if you want each subject as its own file, click "Separate", pick the components you want, and download a ZIP of individual PNGs.

What you can do

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. Forever. The site has no ads or trackers. There is no paid tier today; if a Pro tier ships in the future, it will add features on top of the free tier (which will keep working as-is).
Where does my image go?
Nowhere. All processing — including the AI inference — happens on your own device. The site has no upload endpoint; it can't receive your image even if it tried.
Why is the first AI run slow?
When you click the AI background-removal button, the AI model (~470MB) is downloaded into your browser cache. A consent dialog appears on first use. Subsequent runs load instantly from cache.
Which browsers work?
AI background removal needs WebGPU — Chrome, Edge, or Firefox latest. Safari can use the manual editing features but not the AI step (yet).
